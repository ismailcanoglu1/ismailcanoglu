About

I'm a mathematician by training and a PhD candidate in computer science, working on where quantum computing meets machine learning.

My path started with a BSc in Mathematics at Dokuz Eylül University, where my graduation project looked at quantum field theory and analytical methods for hard equations. An MSc in Computer Science followed, with a thesis on a fuzzy logic–based decision support system for early risk assessment of skin diseases.

Since 2021 I have worked in data roles across energy, gaming, HR technology and e-commerce: forecasting, product analytics, decision support systems and advertising data. I like turning an abstract question into an experiment, then into something a team can use.
